This is a newly written Stata/Mata implementation of established statistical
constructions. The SAS sample below is a mathematical algorithm reference.
No SAS macro source is included. No affiliation, endorsement, or vendor
executable validation is claimed. See docs/methods.md for source-to-method
mapping, including the distinction between the two 1984 publications.

Edwards, Donald G., and Hsu, Jason C. (1983). Multiple Comparisons with the Best Treatment. Journal of the American Statistical Association, 78(384), 965-971. https://doi.org/10.2307/2288211

Edwards, Donald G., and Hsu, Jason C. (1984). Corrigenda: Multiple Comparisons with the Best Treatment. Journal of the American Statistical Association, 79(388), 965. https://doi.org/10.2307/2288771

Hsu, Jason C. (1981). Simultaneous Confidence Intervals for all Distances from the "Best". The Annals of Statistics, 9(5), 1026-1034. https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1176345582

Hsu, Jason C. (1984). Constrained Simultaneous Confidence Intervals for Multiple Comparisons with the Best. The Annals of Statistics, 12(3), 1136-1144. https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1176346732

Hsu, Jason C. (1996). Multiple Comparisons: Theory and Methods. London: Chapman & Hall. ISBN 0-412-98281-1. https://www.asc.ohio-state.edu/hsu.1/book.html

Tukey, John W. (1949). Comparing Individual Means in the Analysis of Variance. Biometrics, 5(2), 99-114. https://doi.org/10.2307/3001913

Kramer, Clyde Young (1956). Extension of Multiple Range Tests to Group Means with Unequal Numbers of Replications. Biometrics, 12(3), 307-310. https://doi.org/10.2307/3001469

SAS Institute Inc. (1996). MCB: Multiple Comparisons with the Best. SAS Sample Library; support: Randy Tobias; updated 16 January 1996. https://support.sas.com/documentation/onlinedoc/stat/ex_code/132/mcb.html
